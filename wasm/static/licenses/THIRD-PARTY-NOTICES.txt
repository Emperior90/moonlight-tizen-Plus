MOONLIGHT PLUS: LICENCE AND THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
===============================================

Moonlight Plus is an unofficial fork of Moonlight Tizen for Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen. It streams games, programs
or the whole desktop from a PC with Sunshine (or another GameStream host) to the TV. It is not made or supported by the
Moonlight Tizen project, the Moonlight project or Samsung.

Moonlight Plus is free software: you can redistribute it and/or modify it under the terms of the GNU General Public
License as published by the Free Software Foundation, version 3 of the License (GPL-3.0), like Moonlight Tizen.

Moonlight Plus is distributed in the hope that it will be useful, but WITHOUT ANY WARRANTY; without even the implied
warranty of MERCHANTABILITY or FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE. See the GNU General Public License for more details.
The full text of the GPL-3.0 is in the file LICENSE at the top of the app package (the .wgt file) and at the top of
the source repository.

Source code: https://github.com/Emperior90/moonlight-tizen-Plus
Each release links the commit it was built from. docs/BUILD.md in the repository lists everything that the build
downloads (the SDKs and the sources of OpenSSL, curl and zlib).

CODE FROM OTHER FORKS OF MOONLIGHT TIZEN (GPL-3.0)
--------------------------------------------------

Moonlight Plus contains code and translations ported from these forks. They are licensed under the GPL-3.0 like
Moonlight Plus, and the source files name the commits that the code comes from.

  fidoboy, moonlight-tizen-ac3 (https://github.com/fidoboy/moonlight-tizen-ac3): the patch of the Embind std::string
    code that the build applies (tools/patch-embind-string.js, based on a patch by mmadej20), the key combinations of
    the Stream Menu (wasm/platform/menus.js) and the German and French translations that the translations of Moonlight
    Plus started from. The Stream Menu on Play/Pause and the statistics graph follow this fork.
  mmadej20, moonlight-tizen-modern (https://github.com/mmadej20/moonlight-tizen-modern): the request limiter of the box
    art (static/js/utils.js). The diagnostic log, its redaction, the heartbeat of a stream and checks of the pairing
    follow this fork.
  samirmartins, moonlight-tizen (https://github.com/samirmartins/moonlight-tizen): the subnet broadcast address of
    Wake-on-LAN (static/js/utils.js). The session report, the frame interval statistics, the button mapping, the
    recently played apps and the rounds of Wake-on-LAN packets follow this fork.
  Diogo8500, moonlight-tizen (https://github.com/Diogo8500/moonlight-tizen, pull request 177 of Moonlight Tizen): the
    Stream Menu and the key diagnostics, which reached Moonlight Plus through the fork of fidoboy.
  waresz, moonlight-tizen (https://github.com/waresz/moonlight-tizen): the stick calibration.

Portions Copyright (C) fidoboy, mmadej20, samirmartins, Diogo8500, waresz and the contributors of their forks.
