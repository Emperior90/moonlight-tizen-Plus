**New since 1.19.101:**
- Statistics: Off, Compact (one line) or Full, stepped with YELLOW
- New: *Alt + Enter* in the Stream Menu, *Continuous audio from the host* setting
- One silent retry when no video arrives
- Safer pairing: the PC must know the PIN
- Wake PC: a stronger request, and the dialog no longer hangs
- Fixed: a freeze after a UI error, lost mouse movements, slow stop while starting

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt`. Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**Install** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

**Moonlight S90C (v1.19.0-s90c.1)** won't announce 1.19.102: install it by hand.

Tested with simulated TVs, hosts and controllers.
