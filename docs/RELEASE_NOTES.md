**New since 2.3.0:**
- A double press of OK or A no longer changes a setting; a controller press counts once
- *Surround audio quality* is *Efficient* unless chosen; 7.1 plays right; Web Audio is stereo
- *Prioritize sound on Wi-Fi* (Audio Settings)
- A PC shows its real state at start, not ONLINE or Running from the last time
- A sturdier streaming engine, fewer keyframe requests on Wi-Fi, safer against fake PCs
- Lighter statistics, a fuller log, hints when the sound overloads the TV or drifts
- Tizen 9 and 10: keep *Ultra low latency mode* off; one warning if a TV update left it on

**Which file?** `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` on Tizen 9 and newer, `Moonlight.wgt` on Tizen 5.5 to 8.

**Install 2.3.1** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

Tested on simulated TVs, hosts, controllers.
