**New since 2.3.2:**
- In a stream the arrows and OK reach the PC from the second press, without RETURN
- CH+, CH- and color keys no longer reach the PC; B of a controller cancels a start
- A PC that dropped the TV (e.g. after *Unpair All*) shows *Unpaired*, and OK pairs it again
- Video has a reading thread of its own, and the sound catches up after a Wi-Fi stall
- *Quit app on the PC* in the Stream Menu asks first, and Exit and Restart open on *Cancel*
- Long messages stay 5 to 15 seconds by their length and make room for the statistics

**Which file?** `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` on Tizen 9 and newer, `Moonlight.wgt` on Tizen 5.5 to 8.

**Install 2.3.5** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

Tested on simulated TVs and hosts; video, keys, dialogs, controllers and sound need a TV.
