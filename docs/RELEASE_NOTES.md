**New since 2.3.2:**
- In a stream the remote's arrows and OK reach the PC without RETURN; TV-only keys no longer press keys on the PC
- A keyboard's Ctrl + Alt + Shift + S no longer turns the keyboard off; a mouse scrolls sideways
- A PC that no longer accepts the TV (e.g. after Unpair All in Sunshine) shows Unpaired and pairs again; also while it runs a game

**Which file?** `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` on Tizen 9 and newer, `Moonlight.wgt` on Tizen 5.5 to 8.

**Install 2.3.3** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

Tested on simulated TVs and hosts; the keys, the keyboard and the mouse still need a real TV.
