**New since 2.2.0:**
- 144 FPS is gone: the video player of the TV never started it; a saved 144 FPS becomes 120 or 60 FPS
- Fixed: some controllers on Tizen 5.5 TVs (2020) did nothing in a game and were missing in the menus
- The README credits Moonlight Tizen, its maintainer and its contributors

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` (keep *Ultra low latency mode* off). Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**Install** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

**Moonlight S90C (v1.19.0-s90c.1)** won't announce 2.2.1: install it by hand.

Tested on simulated TVs, hosts, controllers.
