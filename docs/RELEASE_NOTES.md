**New since 2.2.1:**
- Smoother motion after packet loss and in games below the stream rate
- *Refresh picture* in the Stream Menu clears a broken picture
- Better H.264 above 1080p; System information shows the TV firmware
- A longer guide: TV picture, HDR calibration, network and FEC

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` (keep *Ultra low latency mode* off). Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**Install** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

**Moonlight S90C (v1.19.0-s90c.1)** won't announce 2.3.0: install it by hand.

Tested on simulated TVs, hosts, controllers.
