**New since 2.3.2:**
- In a stream the arrows and OK reach the PC from the second press, without RETURN
- CH+, CH-, Source and the color keys no longer press keys on the PC
- A PC that dropped the TV (e.g. after *Unpair All*), also during a game, shows *Unpaired*, and OK pairs it again
- Ctrl + Alt + Shift + S no longer turns the keyboard off, and a mouse scrolls sideways
- Long messages stay 5 to 15 seconds by their length and make room for the statistics
- The log tells how fast the app reads video

**Which file?** `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` on Tizen 9 and newer, `Moonlight.wgt` on Tizen 5.5 to 8.

**Install 2.3.4** over the old app (settings and pairing normally stay), or next to the regular Moonlight app (pair your PC once).

Tested on simulated TVs and hosts; keys, mouse, pairing, messages and video need a TV.
