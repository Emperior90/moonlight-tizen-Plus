**Moonlight Plus 2.1.0 (pre-release).** Installs over 2.0.0 and earlier; settings and pairing normally stay.

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` (keep the app's *Game mode* switch off). Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**First install?** It goes next to the regular Moonlight app; pair your PC once.

**New since 2.0.0:**
- Languages: German, Spanish and French
- Stream Menu: change the bitrate in a game, type text on the PC
- A statistics graph, in a corner you choose
- App list: favorites, recently played, host order
- Last stream report, diagnostic log, input test, button mapping
- Starts no longer wait for box art; hints for Apollo and Vibepollo

Tested with simulated TVs, hosts and controllers so far.
