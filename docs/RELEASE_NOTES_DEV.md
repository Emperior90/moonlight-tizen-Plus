**Moonlight Plus 2.1.0 (pre-release).** Installs over 1.19.101, the 2.0.0 pre-release and earlier; settings and pairing normally stay.

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` (keep the app's *Game mode* switch off). Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**First install?** It goes next to the regular Moonlight app; pair your PC once.

**New since 1.19.101:**
- Languages: German, Spanish and French; favorites and recently played in the app list
- Stream Menu: change the bitrate in a game, type text on the PC, *Alt + Enter*
- Statistics: Off, Compact or Full with a graph; safer pairing; Wake PC no longer hangs
- Last stream report, diagnostic log, input test, button mapping per controller
- Starts no longer wait for box art; Apollo hints; one retry without video; fewer freezes

Tested with simulated TVs, hosts and controllers so far.
