**Moonlight Plus 2.0.0 (pre-release).** Installs over 1.19.101 and earlier; settings and pairing normally stay.

**Which file?** Tizen 9 and newer: `Moonlight-ForceGM.wgt` (keep the app's *Game mode* switch off). Tizen 5.5 to 8: `Moonlight.wgt`.

**First install?** It goes next to the regular Moonlight app; pair your PC once.

**New since 1.19.101:**
- Statistics: Off, Compact (one line) or Full, stepped with YELLOW
- New: *Alt + Enter* in the Stream Menu, *Continuous audio from the host* setting
- One silent retry when no video arrives
- Safer pairing: the PC must know the PIN
- Wake PC: a stronger request, and the dialog no longer hangs
- Fixed: a freeze after a UI error, lost mouse movements, slow stop while starting

Tested with simulated TVs, hosts and controllers so far.
